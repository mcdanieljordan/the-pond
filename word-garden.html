Teacher workflow and FAQs
  1. Open Spelling tests, create or import a 10–12-word draft, then review and save it.
  2. Add the story, optional questions, MP3 narration, and optional story images. Preview and print the worksheet.
  3. Open Lists, select and review the words students may use, then activate that selection.
  4. Choose practice settings in Lists, add nickname-only learners in Students, and let learners use Learn, Practice, and Review.
  5. Use Results for device-only progress. Parent/family links stay local until publishing is completed.

Do I need sign-in?

Sign-in loads the read-only reference library. Test drafts, learner nicknames, recordings, and images stay on this device, so export a backup and keep source files.

What should I do first?

Create and review a test, then make an approved word selection before opening student practice.

Can a parent use a link?

Family links are local in this version. Publishing a shareable parent experience requires the planned hosted publishing step.

          How these early progress states work

          Seed means a word was introduced. Sprout means it was recalled during learning. Plant, Flower, and Established Flower need unassisted retrieval on later days, delayed recall, and later maintenance evidence. Due dates never remove an earned stage.
